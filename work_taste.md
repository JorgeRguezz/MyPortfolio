# Work Taste

## Career direction

I am pursuing **applied, product-facing AI roles** where I can turn advanced AI
capabilities into reliable software that solves real operational problems. My
strength is at the intersection of AI engineering, product delivery, and
customer-facing implementation - not purely academic research.

## Target roles

- Applied AI Engineer / AI Engineer
- Forward Deployed Engineer
- ML Engineer focused on RAG, agents, multimodal systems, or AI automation
- Solutions Engineer / AI Solutions Architect, working closely with customers
  and operational teams
- Product engineer at an early-stage or growth-stage AI startup

## Ideal working environment

I do my best work in high-ownership environments where I can take a problem
end-to-end: understand the workflow, design the AI and data pipeline, build and
deploy the product, then iterate from user feedback and measured outcomes.

My experience supports this direction:

- At **NTT DATA**, I worked on enterprise automation and intelligent document
  processing for banking workflows.
- At **MOTIVATE-XR**, I built multimodal retrieval and knowledge systems using
  vision-language models, speech recognition, LLMs, RAG, knowledge graphs, and
  agentic tooling.
- Through my GitHub projects, I have built multimodal video-analysis and RAG
  systems with practical deployment, evaluation, and user-facing components.
- Through product and community leadership, I have demonstrated that I can work
  effectively in small, high-agency teams and help bring initiatives from idea
  to launch.

## Location and team preferences

I am positioned for opportunities in the **United States**, particularly while
studying at Illinois Tech in Chicago, as well as international and remote teams.
My background and network in Madrid and Spain make me well suited to
cross-border organizations and globally distributed teams.

I can succeed in large technology companies, consultancies, and startups. My
strongest preference is for teams with real ownership, direct exposure to users
or customers, rapid learning cycles, and a clear path from experimentation to a
maintained product.

## Companies and company types I am targeting

| Category | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| Frontier AI and AI platforms | OpenAI, Anthropic, Google DeepMind, Mistral AI, Microsoft AI |
| Applied AI and forward deployment | Palantir, Anduril, Vannevar Labs, Scale AI |
| AI product and knowledge systems | Glean, Hebbia, Harvey, Dust |
| AI infrastructure and data platforms | Databricks, Snowflake, Hugging Face, LangChain |
| High-impact startups | Teams building multimodal products, enterprise agents, document intelligence, or AI workflow automation |

The defining criterion is not simply that a company uses AI. I want to work at
organizations that **deploy AI into consequential real-world workflows**, keep
engineers close to customers and domain experts, and empower teams to own the
full cycle of defining, building, shipping, and improving a product.
